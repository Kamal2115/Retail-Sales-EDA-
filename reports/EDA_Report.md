# Retail Sales EDA Report

## Executive Summary

This report summarizes the exploratory analysis performed on the retail
sales dataset.

## Sales Performance

Monthly and quarterly sales analysis was performed to identify high and
low-performing periods.

## Customer Behaviour

Customer behaviour was analyzed by:

- Age group
- Gender
- Average transaction value

## Product Performance

Product categories were compared based on:

- Quantity sold
- Total revenue
- Average transaction value

## Key Insights

1. Identify the strongest sales period.
2. Identify the highest-value customer segment.
3. Identify the highest-revenue category.
4. Identify differences between sales volume and revenue.

## Recommendations

1. Target high-value customer segments.
2. Optimize inventory according to seasonal demand.
3. Increase investment in high-revenue categories.
4. Use cross-selling and upselling to increase transaction value.
5. Personalize marketing campaigns using customer demographics.
